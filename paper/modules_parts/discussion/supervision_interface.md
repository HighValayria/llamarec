<!-- PARAGRAPH: discussion.semantics.p01 -->
<!-- EVIDENCE: C1,C2,M1,M4 -->

**EN**

Recommendation capability is meaningful only relative to a prediction question. Y estimates rating-derived preference, whereas N selects the next observed interaction. Native preference discrimination and bridge ranking therefore assess different behavior: a model can be useful for preference while being less suited to next-event selection.

**ZH**

推荐能力只有相对于具体预测问题才有明确含义。Y估计评分产生的偏好，N选择下一观测交互。原生偏好判别与桥接排序因此评估不同的行为：模型可以适合偏好判断，却不一定适合下一事件选择。

<!-- PARAGRAPH: discussion.semantics.p02 -->
<!-- EVIDENCE: C1,M1,M4 -->

**EN**

A benchmark's target, eligible events, prompt, and scoring rule jointly determine the capability reflected by its score. Reporting native and bridge results exposes distinctions that one ranking number can hide and keeps the Y/N contrast attached to complete formulations rather than a single semantic cause.

**ZH**

基准的目标、合法事件、提示和评分规则共同决定分数反映的能力。并列报告原生与桥接结果，可以呈现单一排序数值容易掩盖的区别，并将Y/N对照限定为完整形式比较，而非单一语义原因。
