<!-- PARAGRAPH: discussion.protocol.p01 -->
<!-- EVIDENCE: C5,C6,M5 -->

**EN**

At 96k, k20 exposes a larger N advantage than standard k5, whereas k50 is smaller than k20 and not uniformly larger than k5. A standard-k5 result alone therefore cannot characterize specialist performance retained under other candidate conditions. The relation is protocol-conditioned, not monotonic in candidate count.

**ZH**

在96k时，k20呈现比标准k5更大的N优势，k50则小于k20且并非总大于k5。因此，仅凭标准k5结果不能刻画其他候选条件下保留的专家表现；该关系受协议限定，不随候选数量单调变化。

<!-- PARAGRAPH: discussion.protocol.p02 -->
<!-- EVIDENCE: C5,C6,M5 -->

**EN**

Fixed candidates within a protocol support paired model comparison, while cross-protocol differences describe sensitivity to evaluated conditions. Because the sets are independently constructed and non-nested, count, composition, sampling, and difficulty change jointly. This separates within-protocol evidence from unsupported candidate-count causality.

**ZH**

协议内部固定候选支持模型配对比较，跨协议差异则描述对评估条件的敏感性。由于候选集独立构造且非嵌套，数量、组成、采样和难度共同变化。这一区分保留协议内证据，并排除未经支持的候选数量因果解释。
