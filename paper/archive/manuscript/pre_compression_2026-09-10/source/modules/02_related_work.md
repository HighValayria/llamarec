**EN**

These strands establish task diversity, shared recommendation modeling, data-dependent performance, and candidate-sensitive evaluation in partially overlapping settings. Our study connects them through linked comparisons of model relationships within a shared LLM adaptation framework. The main question is how specialist-multitask relationships change with cumulative per-task supervision; the sequential-baseline trajectory and alternative candidate conditions provide complementary comparisons and boundaries. Supervision formulation defines the prediction questions to which these conclusions apply. The following formulation and methods specify those interfaces and comparison conditions.

**ZH**

这些研究脉络已在部分重合的设置中考察任务多样性、共享推荐建模、数据依赖的表现与候选敏感的评估。本文在共同的 LLM 适配框架内，通过相互关联的模型关系比较将它们连接起来。核心问题是专家与多任务关系如何随累计每任务监督变化；序列基线轨迹和不同候选条件提供互补比较与适用边界。监督形式则界定这些结论针对的预测问题。下文的问题定义与方法进一步说明这些接口和比较条件。
