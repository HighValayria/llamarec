**EN**

The two datasets serve complementary roles in the study. MovieLens provides the detailed variation in exposure and candidate protocol needed to examine how model comparisons change, while Amazon tests the ranking directions at its available operating points. The present evidence thus combines a detailed within-domain analysis with an external directional check.
