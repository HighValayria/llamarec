# Final Research Questions

RQ1 asks whether preference supervision and next-item supervision teach LLMs
different recommendation capabilities.

RQ2 asks whether one multi-task LLM can retain both capabilities, and what
specialization cost unification introduces.

RQ3 asks how candidate-set difficulty affects conclusions drawn from LLM
recommendation evaluation.

RQ4 asks how recommendation-tuned LLMs compare with SASRec across different
sequential-supervision exposure regimes.

RQ5 asks which MovieLens-1M findings directionally reproduce on Amazon Musical
Instruments.

These RQs are frozen for Draft v0. New RQs require evidence that the current
five-question structure cannot cover an essential manuscript claim.
